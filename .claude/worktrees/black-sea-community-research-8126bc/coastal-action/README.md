# Coastal Action

Our challenge is fish video monitoring for Coastal Action, an environmental charity in Mahone Bay, Nova Scotia. The product is [TideCount](../tidecount/README.md).

**Requirements, design and research now live in [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/allenyan513/RBC_HUBHACKS_2026/issues), not in this repo.** Start with the [PRD](https://github.com/allenyan513/RBC_HUBHACKS_2026/issues/13) and the [technical design](https://github.com/allenyan513/RBC_HUBHACKS_2026/issues/14).
